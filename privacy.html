Privacy notice

How Waste Smart handles personal information

Waste Smart is delivered by Gilded Age Consulting, the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We keep what the platform needs to run your kitchen's costing, and little else.

What we hold

InformationWhy
Business name, contact person, business emailTo set up and run your account and send your verification code, invoices and service notices.
Administrator names and sign-in emails (Gilded Age and group administrators)To control who can see and manage sites.
Demo requests (business name, email, optional contact)To review your request and send your demo verification code.
Your site's measurements, costs and recipesTo produce your reports. This is business data, not personal information about your customers.
An administrator activity log (who did what, and when)Security and accountability.

Kitchen staff are not given accounts and are not identified in the platform. Files you choose to import for a demo stay on your own device until you load them.

Who sees it

Only the people you authorise at your business or group, and Gilded Age administrators who support your account. We use an email provider to send messages and a hosting provider to run the platform. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. The website no longer loads fonts or scripts from third parties.

How long we keep it

  • Active clients: while the service runs and for the period the law requires for financial records.
  • Demo requests that never go ahead: removed automatically after 90 days.
  • Erased clients: removed from the live system on request. Encrypted backups age out on the normal backup cycle.

How it is protected

  • Passwords and codes are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Optional two-factor sign-in for Gilded Age and group administrators.
  • Backups are encrypted and checked regularly.
  • Connections use HTTPS, with security headers set on the platform.
  • Administrator actions are logged.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, receive a copy in an open format, or have it erased. We can export or erase a whole site on request, and confirm the erasure in writing. If a security incident affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Contact

Write to the Information Officer, Gilded Age Consulting, through www.gildedac.co.za. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.
