How it works

Connected once, then it runs on its own

There is no scanning, photographing or manual capture. Devices report straight into the platform, with manual entry kept only as a backup.

We connect your equipment

Gilded Age sets up your site and issues a secure key for each scale, meter and POS connection, with ready-made adapters for common devices.

Data arrives automatically

Scales report every weighing. Gas, water and electricity meters refresh every one to two hours. Sales, write-offs and staff shifts come from your POS.

You see cost, profit and leakage

Readings are matched to the hour they happened, set against sales, and turned into a short list of actions. Reports and a Power BI feed are built in.

What it measures

Every cost driver in the kitchen, on one platform

Kitchen wasteAll food thrown away, preparation and plate together, by electronic scale.
RecyclingPlastic, paper and cardboard, glass and cans, weighed by material.
Used cooking oilVolume collected and the recovery value.
GasCylinder level or flow, converted to usage and cost.

WaterMeter readings with tariffs applied.
Sales and voidsHourly sales, write-offs with reasons, and staff shifts from the POS.
Waste against salesWaste per cover compared with that weekday's usual level, with no recipes needed.
