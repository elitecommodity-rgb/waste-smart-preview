      Connected devices

      How often should meters report?

      Gas, electricity and water meters send a running total every 1 to 2 hours. Waste Smart works out what was used between two readings and spreads it over the hours it covers, so a reading at 00:30 is shared between yesterday and today instead of landing on one day. If a meter goes quiet for longer than 2.5 times its interval, the device shows as late and the catch-up is marked as an estimate. Set each meter's interval when you add it.

      Every hour
      Best detail. Right for electricity and water on a smart meter or logger.
      Every 2 hours
      Fine for gas and for loggers on weak signal. Hourly profile gets coarser.
